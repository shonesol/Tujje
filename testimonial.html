TRAVEL TIPS

Before You Visit Uganda

🎒 What to Pack

Bring comfortable hiking shoes, lightweight clothing, sunscreen, insect repellent and a waterproof jacket.

📷 Photography

Carry a camera with extra batteries and memory cards to capture Uganda's incredible wildlife and scenery.

🌍 Best Time to Visit

Uganda is a year-round destination, but June–September and December–February offer excellent safari conditions.
